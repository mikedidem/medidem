Scientific ML · Flagship

Conservation Diagnostics for Deep Learning Groundwater Surrogates

A surrogate can match a groundwater simulator to R² > 0.999 and still violate the physics it was trained on. I built a diagnostic framework that scores CNN, ConvLSTM and physics-informed surrogates on accuracy and on governing-equation residuals, boundary behavior and mass balance against MODFLOW-2005.

All six models pass a conventional accuracy screen, yet the data-driven surrogates leave PDE residuals 84–289× the numerical baseline. The physics-informed model produced substantially smaller residuals while keeping mass-balance error within 1.5%.

Deployed system · Decision support

CHIRRP: Hydro-AI for Participatory Water Planning

A deployed app where stakeholders ask groundwater questions in plain language ("reduce pumping by 15%") and get physics-informed predictions, cross-checked against MODFLOW-2005, with regulation-grounded context via RAG.

Full head field in 0.11 s vs. 26.4 s for MODFLOW (≈238× on CPU), at R² 0.98 across 10 pumping rates. Live on Hugging Face.

GeoAI · Computer vision

Drainage-Crossing Detection from High-Resolution DEMs

Culverts and bridges under roads are missing from elevation models, so derived stream networks break at every road. I developed deep-learning classifiers and detectors that locate these crossings from 1-m LiDAR terrain data.

97.9% accuracy (F1 0.979) classifying crossings with EfficientNetV2 on HRDEM-derived terrain layers, with Grad-CAM to explain each prediction.

Current research In progress

Earth Foundation Models and Neural Operators

Foundation-model transfer. Do Earth foundation-model embeddings (AlphaEarth Foundations) encode transferable drainage-crossing structure, or only regional appearance? I'm testing transfer across regions for spatially implicit infrastructure mapping.

Neural operators. Finite-volume, physics-informed neural operators as fast surrogates for transient unconfined groundwater flow, with an analysis of the accuracy, cost and physical-consistency tradeoffs.

About

Background

I'm a PhD researcher in Geographic Information Systems and Environmental Modeling at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. My work combines spatial and spatiotemporal modeling, remote sensing and computer vision, and neural surrogate modeling for environmental applications.

Before my graduate work I spent five years in applied geospatial roles, from surveying to enterprise GIS and UAV inspection at a power utility. In 2025 I interned as a GIS Specialist at CDM Smith, working on AI-based wetland delineation and Bayesian PFAS modeling.
