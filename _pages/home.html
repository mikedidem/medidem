Research interests

Four connected themes

Geospatial AI and scientific ML methods, applied to Earth and environmental systems: groundwater, hydrography, infrastructure and land surface.

01Geospatial AI & Spatial Intelligence

Developing computational approaches to understand spatial relationships, geographic patterns, and variation across places, with applications in infrastructure and environmental systems.

02Computer Vision & Multimodal AI

Developing and evaluating methods that extract and combine information from imagery, terrain, language, and other data sources, including ongoing research with vision-language models.

03Scientific Machine Learning & Simulation

Integrating machine learning with numerical simulation and physical knowledge to investigate predictive accuracy, physical consistency, generalization, and model behavior.

04AI Systems & Scientific Applications

Building reproducible computational workflows and decision-support applications, while exploring agentic AI for tool integration, workflow automation, and reliable scientific execution.
