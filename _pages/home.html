Conservation Diagnostics for Groundwater Surrogates

A model can predict groundwater levels accurately and still violate physical laws. I developed a framework that evaluates predictive accuracy alongside conservation, boundary behavior and governing-equation residuals.

Research interests

Four connected themes

Geospatial AI and scientific ML methods, applied to Earth and environmental systems: groundwater, hydrography, infrastructure and land surface.

01

Geospatial AI

Spatial and spatiotemporal prediction from sparse, heterogeneous geospatial data, and whether learned representations transfer across regions.

02

Remote Sensing & Computer Vision

Feature extraction, detection and mapping from imagery and terrain data, with explainability to show what each prediction is based on.

03

Scientific Machine Learning

Neural surrogates for expensive environmental simulations, judged on physical consistency, not just fit.

04

Reproducible ML Systems

Evaluation and benchmarking others can rerun, and decision-support tools that put models in front of the people who use them.

Background

I'm a PhD researcher in Geographic Information Systems and Environmental Modeling at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. Before graduate school I spent five years in applied geospatial work: surveying, enterprise GIS and UAV inspection at a power utility.
