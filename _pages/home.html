I work at the intersection of geospatial AI and scientific machine learning. My research focuses on spatial and spatiotemporal prediction, remote sensing and computer vision for feature extraction and mapping, and neural surrogate modeling for environmental simulation. I translate complex physical and geospatial problems into reproducible machine-learning systems and decision-support tools.

Research

Four connected themes

Geospatial AI and scientific ML methods, applied to Earth and environmental systems: groundwater, hydrography, infrastructure and land surface.

01

Geospatial AI

Spatial and spatiotemporal prediction from heterogeneous geospatial data, and whether learned representations transfer across regions.

02

Remote Sensing & Computer Vision

Feature extraction, detection and mapping from imagery and terrain data, with explainability to show why a model fires.

03

Scientific Machine Learning

Neural surrogates for expensive environmental simulations, judged on physical consistency, not just fit.

04

Reproducible ML Systems

Evaluation and benchmarking others can rerun, and decision-support tools that put models in front of the people who use them.

About

Background

I'm a PhD researcher in Geographic Information Systems and Environmental Modeling at Southern Illinois University Carbondale. Before graduate school I spent five years in applied geospatial work: surveying, enterprise GIS and UAV inspection at a power utility. In 2025 I interned as a GIS Specialist at CDM Smith, working on AI-based wetland delineation and Bayesian PFAS modeling.
