Transferability of Earth Foundation Model Embeddings for Spatially Implicit Infrastructure Mapping

Global embeddings are not global guarantees.

RoleLead author
PresentedCaGIS 2026, St. Louis
StatusOngoing research
DataAlphaEarth embeddings · LiDAR DEM

Problem

Question: AlphaEarth embeddings cover the whole planet. Does a crossing model built on them still work outside the region it was trained in?

Test design

Train in Nebraska. Test unchanged in Illinois, North Dakota and California: no retraining, no local tuning.

Inputs: DEM terrain variables, AlphaEarth embeddings (64-D), or both. Models: XGBoost and a CNN.

Results

PR-AUC, higher is better.

ConfigurationNebraska (in-domain)IllinoisNorth DakotaCalifornia
XGBoost · DEM0.790.470.570.60
XGBoost · AlphaEarth0.850.650.670.48
XGBoost · DEM + AlphaEarth0.890.650.660.55
CNN · DEM0.990.940.570.52
CNN · AlphaEarth0.880.680.710.52
CNN · DEM + AlphaEarth0.990.950.540.48
  • No configuration transfers uniformly. The best choice changes with the target region.
  • AlphaEarth helps in some places and hurts in others, depending on the model and the region.

Next step: applicability, not just prediction

Can we tell whether a model will work in a new region before any labels exist there? Applicability maps, such as the area of applicability (Meyer & Pebesma, 2021), are the next step.
