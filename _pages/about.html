About

Background & Experience

I'm a PhD researcher at Southern Illinois University Carbondale working at the intersection of geospatial AI and scientific machine learning. My research combines spatial and spatiotemporal modeling, remote sensing and computer vision, and neural surrogate modeling for environmental applications. It builds on five years of applied geospatial work in surveying, utilities and engineering consulting.
